# Step 2: consistent use-case categories

Approved for publication by the owner, 20 October 2026. Step 6 mobile film changes are excluded.

## Grouping

Replace the mixed audiences/capability grouping with three situations in professional client work:

1. Client onboarding: forward ID records and agreements, then file them under the client.
2. Recurring client work: organise returns, statements and reports using the existing four filing details.
3. Client requests: ask for and retrieve a document through the supplied WhatsApp workflow.

Each card has a meaningful icon, a situation-based title and a concrete description. These use the already supplied forwarding, filing and retrieval behaviour; no classification, compliance, tax-filing or case-management capabilities are added.

## Presentation

The three cards have equal weight on desktop and stack as consistent icon/text rows on tablets and phones. Keep the approved Step 4 frames, existing reveal behaviour and light/dark/system themes.

Confirmed security and document controls remain in their dedicated section. The wider About revision is Step 3 and mobile film viewing is Step 6.
